The quiet art of slow travel

by Mira Okafor · May 14

The best moments of a trip are ephemeral: steam off a morning cup, a street musician's last song, light sliding across old stone. You can't schedule them. You can only stay long enough to notice.

We rush from landmark to landmark as if the map were a checklist. Slow travel asks for the opposite: one town, one café, one long walk at dusk, until a place starts to feel like yours.

So book fewer stops. Leave whole afternoons empty. Let the train be late.
